# Known gaps

STAFFPORTAL_SITES_INCOMPLETE

## SITE-01 · Critical · Zero disclosure

All fixtures remain shipped in the client bundle; no whole-product field/action/file/cache certification. Production must never use this delivery model.

## SITE-02 · High · Workflow depth

Recruitment, printing, bakery, logistics and properties do not yet have all requested linked event/accounting flows.

## SITE-03 · High · Builders

Forms have one configurable field; workflow graph editor lacks multi-node authoring; advanced conditions, scheduling and approvals remain partial.

## SITE-04 · High · Imports and exports

CSV/XLSX import workflow, bulk editing and PDF/XLSX exports remain incomplete.

## SITE-05 · High · Files

Versioning, locking, tags, retention, expiry and byte-governance fixture flows remain partial.

## SITE-06 · High · Administration

Full multi-role lifecycle, policy audience management, scoped self-service configuration and multi-stage approvals remain incomplete.

## SITE-07 · High · Fixture coherence

Receipt/stock targeted conservation passes; many legacy charts still use illustrative independent values.

## SITE-08 · High · Visual/accessibility/performance acceptance

Dimension checks do not establish keyboard/zoom/contrast, every page/state, Core Web Vitals or independent visual acceptance.

## Production integration

Identity, Worker routes, D1 schemas/transactions, R2, providers, background GPS, immutable audit and sync require existing backend mapping. These are intentionally not implemented in this Site.
